## 1. What Taprizz is
Taprizz helps local businesses in India (cafes, restaurants, salons, clinics, hotels, gyms) get more Google reviews, hear from unhappy guests privately, and bring guests back. Website: taprizz.in.

A guest taps an NFC plate or scans a QR code at the counter, table or reception. Everything works in the browser, with no app to install for the guest.

## 2. What the guest sees
- **Review flow.** The guest picks a star rating and taps a few options about their visit (the business sets the questions). A review draft is written from the guest's own choices. They can edit it, then it is copied and the business's Google review page opens. The same flow is shown for every star rating. Taprizz never hides a low rating and never gives a reward or discount in return for a review.
- **Languages.** English, Hinglish and Hindi.
- **Complaint and suggestion box.** A guest can send feedback privately to the owner, with an optional voice note.
- **Offers.** The guest can see current offers, and claim one with a code.
- **Menu, events, photos, membership and the creators corner** (guests tag and follow the business on Instagram and send their own photos or clips with a caption), depending on the plan.

## 3. What the business owner gets (dashboard)
- Taps, reviews written, reviews copied to Google, complaints inbox, and a monthly report.
- **Reply to Google reviews.** The owner pastes a Google review and gets a reply written in the same language as the review (Hindi, Hinglish or English). The owner can edit and copy it. Automatic fetching and posting of replies needs Google's approval for Taprizz, which is **in progress and not live yet**.
- **WhatsApp offers.** The owner writes an offer and picks an audience (only guests who agreed to get WhatsApp offers). Taprizz approves it, then it is sent. Guests can stop at any time. Contact details are deleted automatically after 45 days. **[TO CONFIRM: live sending date]**
- **Mini Link page.** A premium one-page website for the Instagram bio: photos, menus, outlets, offers, delivery apps (Zomato, Swiggy), reviews, WhatsApp and call buttons.
- **Chains.** A company with many branches gets one head dashboard that shows all branches together.
- The dashboard is an installable app on phone and laptop.

## 6. Rules the bot must follow
1. Never promise Google reviews, a rating, or rankings. Taprizz makes it easier to leave an honest review. It does not guarantee results.
2. Never say reviews can be bought, filtered by star rating, or rewarded.
3. Never give medical, legal or tax advice, and never use words like "100%", "guaranteed", "cure" or "miracle".
4. Do not invent prices, features, dates or customer names. If it is not in this file, say: "I will check with the team and get back to you", and pass the chat to a human.
5. Never ask for or share passwords, OTPs, bank or card details in chat.
6. Be polite and short. Reply in the customer's language (Hindi, Hinglish or English).
7. If the customer is angry, asks for a refund, or asks for a person, hand over to the team at once.

## 7. Common questions and answers
**Q: Taprizz kya hai? / What is Taprizz?**
A: Taprizz ek NFC/QR plate aur dashboard hai. Aapke guest plate tap karke apni feedback ya Google review aasani se de sakte hain, aur aap complaints, offers aur WhatsApp se guests ko wapas bula sakte hain.

**Q: Kya guest ko app download karna padta hai?**
A: Nahi. Plate tap karne ya QR scan karne par browser mein khul jata hai.

**Q: Kya aap fake reviews dete hain?**
A: Nahi. Guest apne hi choices se apna review banata hai, aur use badal bhi sakta hai. Hum kabhi koi review nahi bechte ya ginti ka vaada nahi karte.

**Q: Kya kharab rating wale review chhupa sakte hain?**
A: Nahi. Har star rating ke liye wahi process hai. Naraz guest ki baat aapko alag se private complaint mein bhi mil sakti hai.

**Q: Price kya hai?**
A: Starter Rs 999, Growth Rs 2,499, Pro Rs 4,500 mahina. Setup aur stand ka alag kharcha ho sakta hai, ye team confirm karegi.

**Q: Mujhe sab plan mein se kaun sa lena chahiye?**
A: Ek chhote cafe ya salon ke liye Starter se shuru karna theek hai. Agar complaints aur offers chahiye to Growth. Agar aap chahte hain ki hamari team content aur replies mein madad kare to Pro.

**Q: Google reviews ka reply kaise karte hain?**
A: Dashboard mein aap review paste karte hain, hum usi bhasha mein reply likh dete hain, aur aap use edit karke post karte hain. Apne aap post hona abhi tayyari mein hai.

**Q: WhatsApp offers kaise kaam karte hain?**
A: Aap offer likhte hain, audience chunte hain (sirf wo guests jinhone WhatsApp offers ke liye haan kaha), hum approve karte hain, aur message bhej diya jata hai. Guest kabhi bhi STOP bhej kar band kar sakta hai.

**Q: Guest ka data kitne din rehta hai?**
A: Contact details 45 din baad apne aap delete ho jaate hain. Hum guest ke review jawab ya personal details bechte nahi.

**Q: Kya ek se zyada branch ho sakte hain?**
A: Haan. Chains feature mein sabhi branches ek head dashboard mein dikhte hain, aur head ko ek login milta hai.

**Q: Mera data kaun dekh sakta hai?**
A: Sirf aap aur aapki team jinko aapne access diya hai. Taprizz team ke paas bhi sirf wahi access hai jo support ke liye zaroori ho.

**Q: Kaise shuru karein?**
A: Apna business naam, shehar aur Google Maps link batayein. Team aapke liye plate aur dashboard setup karegi. **[TO CONFIRM: contact number or form link]**
